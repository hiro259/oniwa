## noswoosh — synthetic Dock-swipe gesture

ONIWA's `Sources/ONIWA/InstantSpaceGesture.swift` adapts the synthetic
Dock-swipe/IOHID event construction from [noswoosh](https://github.com/mmathys/noswoosh).
This component was modified for ONIWA's **on-demand switching only** (no
persistent event tap, no daemon, no system-wide shortcut or gesture takeover).
